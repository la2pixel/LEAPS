This folder contains the result tables behind the numbers and figures in the thesis. Training curves, checkpoints, rollout files and figures are not stored here; they are regenerated by the commands in `run.sh` (steps 4 to 6) and written next to these tables.

Conditions are named as in the thesis: `MPO` and `DEP-MPO` are the backbones, `EMG prior w=...` (`real-LAP-w00`, `real-LAP`, `real-LAP-w05` in the `f2_*` files, for w = 0, 0.1, 0.5) is LEAPS with residual weight w, `null` is the null prior and `untrained` the randomly initialized decoder. The null prior is written as the string `null`, so read these files with `pd.read_csv(path, keep_default_na=False)`.

Checkpoints in the gait and EMG tables (`gait_metrics/`, `biomech_fidelity/`): the scripts read rollouts at the selected checkpoint `t_star` when they exist and otherwise fall back to the last checkpoint. In the stored tables, w = 0, w = 0.5 and the walking H1622 w = 0.1 seed are at `t_star`; MPO, DEP-MPO, the null prior and the other w = 0.1 seeds are at the last checkpoint (20M steps; DEP-MPO H1622 seed 1 at 25M). The return tables in `checkpoint_selection/` always use `t_star`.

`checkpoint_selection/`, from `leaps select-checkpoint`, `leaps reeval-checkpoint` and `python -m leaps.analysis.return_table`:

| file | content |
|---|---|
| `<condition>_<body>[_w..]_<reward>.csv` | one row per seed: selected checkpoint `t_star` (environment steps), training-curve score at `t_star`, mean of the last 10 evaluations. `run_dir` is the path of the run on the training machine. |
| `<...>_reeval.csv` | one row per seed: 100 fresh rollouts at `t_star`: mean, SD, SEM and minimum return, fall percentage. |
| `RETURN_TABLE.csv` | the return table: per reward, body and method, the mean and SD across seeds of the re-evaluated return, and fall percentage. |

`gait_metrics/`:

| file | content |
|---|---|
| `GAIT_METRICS.csv` | `leaps gait-metrics`. One row per body and method, plus a human reference row: speed, cadence, stride time, stance / double-support / flight fraction, joint ranges of motion, peak ground reaction force, peak knee and ankle moment, mean activation, agonist-antagonist activation correlations, left/right symmetry indices, time to fall and fall rate. Units are in the column names; definitions are in the docstring of `src/leaps/scripts/gait_metrics.py`. |
| `FIBER_LENGTH.csv` | `python -m leaps.analysis.fiber_length`. Mean deviation of the normalized fiber length from optimal, and fraction of time on the force-length plateau. |

`biomech_fidelity/`:

| file | content |
|---|---|
| `f2_<body>_tstar_..._captionorder.csv` | `leaps biomech-fidelity --figure f2 --tstar`. Per muscle and condition, the gait-cycle correlation `r` between the simulated activation and the EMG of each of the 22 Camargo subjects (mean, SD, n), the mean correlation between human subjects, and whether `r` lies in the human band. In the H0918 file, the w = 0.5 rows were computed from the 20M checkpoint, before rollouts at the selected checkpoint existed; rerunning the command gives the selected-checkpoint values (for example SOL 0.72 instead of 0.79, RF -0.23 instead of 0.09). |
| `ksweep_h0918.csv` | latent dimension sweep on H0918: per k, seed and muscle, the same correlation and the effort. |
| `KINEMATIC_MATCH_variants.csv` | `leaps kinematic-match --regime variants`. For the prior variants (w = 0, 0.1, 0.5, null prior, untrained decoder), the match of joint angles, joint velocities, joint moments and ground reaction force to the human band, and the joint angle offsets. |
| `KINEMATIC_MATCH_onlyvel.csv` | `leaps kinematic-match --regime onlyvel`. The same for all conditions including MPO and DEP-MPO; the thesis takes the backbone rows from this file. It was computed with an earlier version of the human band, so rerunning the command changes most values by up to 0.03 (and the H0918 w = 0.5 row more, see above). |
| `human_gait_band.npz` | the human band used by `leaps kinematic-match`: mean and SD of joint angles, velocities, moments and ground reaction force of six Camargo subjects, treadmill strides at 1.35 ± 0.25 m/s. Rebuilt from the raw dataset if deleted. |
| `kinematics_correlation.csv` | `python -m leaps.analysis.kinematics_correlation`. Gait-cycle correlation of hip, knee and ankle angle and moment and of the ground reaction force with the human mean, with the human band. |

`synergy_ae_vs_linear/`, from `python -m leaps.analysis.ae_vs_linear_synergies` (22 subjects pooled, four held out):

| file | content |
|---|---|
| `r2_vs_k.csv` | held-out reconstruction R² of PCA, ICA, NMF and the autoencoder (mean of five seeds) for k = 1 to 11. Delete the file to recompute it. The autoencoder value at k = 4 (0.73) does not reproduce: with this code two of its five seeds diverge to NaN, and the other three average 0.77. All other cells reproduce exactly. |
| `per_muscle_reconstruction_r2.csv` | the same per EMG channel. |
| `agonist_antagonist_by_k.csv` | how many of the three agonist-antagonist pairs (ankle, knee, hip) each method represents, per k. |
