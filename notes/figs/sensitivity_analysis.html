Sensitivity to residual weight and latent dimension

The reference paper reports, for Unitree A1, that a full-action-space weight of w = 0.1 looks best overall and a latent dimension of 6 gives the most consistent reward. This page pulls the equivalent evidence from the LEAPS run set for the three musculoskeletal bodies.

Recipe: MPO + latent (emg_lap), AB06 decoder, onlyVelRew, no style reward y-axis: test episode score (= velocity task return, 0–1000) Horizon: 20 M steps (paper: 2 M) Compiled: 2026-08-27

The paper's “higher w for unstable / humanoid bodies” recommendation does not transfer to muscle-actuated control under MPO.

On h0918, w = 0.0 and w = 0.1 both sit at ~999 and are dead stable; w = 0.5 is lower (~975) and visibly noisier, with intermittent drops toward 800. On h1622 the ordering is clean and monotone — w = 0.0 > 0.1 > 0.5 — and the standard deviation grows with w. On h2190 every setting collapses to ~230–270 with no w signal at all (24 % EMG coverage, the noisiest body).

Takeaway for the writeup: under the LEAPS recipe the pure prior or a light residual (w ≤ 0.1) is as good as or better than w = 0.5, and more consistent. This also supersedes the earlier project note that “w = 0.1 breaks down entirely” — that observation came from the pre-fix, full-reward, k = 11 era.

Panel B · latent dimension

Latent dimension k  consistent basis, different figure

The paper's Fig. 9b is an RL-reward-versus-latent-dimension sweep. LEAPS has no matched-recipe equivalent: the clean final_experiments set is entirely k = 6, and the older k = 11 runs differ in decoder (AB06 vs. pooled), reward, and autoencoder recipe, so a k = 6 vs. k = 11 comparison there is confounded. What actually drove the k = 6 choice — and the honest analogue to plot — is the decoder's held-out reconstruction R² against latent dimension.

Diminishing returns set the elbow at k = 6.

R² climbs 0.43 → 0.94 from k = 1 to 6, then flattens: 0.958, 0.968, 0.975, 0.979, 0.982 for k = 7…11. The marginal gain roughly halves at each step from k ≈ 4; k = 6 captures 95.8 % of the k = 11 asymptote, and every dimension beyond 6 buys under 0.02 R² (under 0.01 past k = 8). The paper's “latent = action_dim / 2” rule cannot apply here — the EMG source caps at 11 channels regardless of how many muscles a body has.

What this says for the thesis

  • The w result is a genuine cross-domain finding, not a null: the LAP weight guidance was derived under PPO on torque robots and does not carry to MPO on muscle-actuated bodies. Report it as such.
  • The w panel uses episode_score under onlyVelRew with no style term, so it is a clean task-return metric — directly comparable in spirit to the paper's reported “task reward”.
  • The k choice rests on reconstruction quality, matching how the paper motivates its own latent dimension (a variance / PCA criterion, not an RL sweep). State plainly that the RL-side k sweep at matched recipe was not run.
  • h2190 carries no signal on either axis at this recipe. Its 24 % EMG coverage is the binding constraint, orthogonal to both w and k.
